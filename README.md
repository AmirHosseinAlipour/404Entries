Welcome to SBU

Welcome to SBU is a 2D top-down pixel art game developed in just three weeks for the new students’ welcome ceremony at Shahid Beheshti University.

The game was created by a small team — Mohammad, Mehdi, Amirhossein, Mahan, and Sina — driven by passion, care, and many late-night work sessions ❤️
The initial idea was born on a cold night during a casual conversation between Mohammad, Amirhossein, and Mehdi, and it quickly evolved into a full team project as others joined in the following weeks.

Story

The story follows a newly accepted student who has just been admitted to Shahid Beheshti University and is excited to begin the registration process.
However, upon visiting the education office, the student is informed that their documents are incomplete.

To complete the enrollment, the student must visit different professors across the faculty, navigating the university environment and experiencing the chaos, humor, and frustration of academic bureaucracy.

Getting accepted was the easy part.
Registering… is a whole different challenge.
